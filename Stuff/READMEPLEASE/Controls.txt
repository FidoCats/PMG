Basic:
WASD - Movement
MouseMove - Look Around
SpaceBar - Jump
LCtrl, C - Crouch
E - Interact
Q - Special Ability
Esc - Menu
M - SpawnMenu
L - MouseLock Toggle
K - HurtBind
LAlt - Kick, Freeze Held Object
G - Throwable
H - Switch Throwable
O - Camera Further
I - Camera Closer
P - Reset Camera Distance
T - Flashlight
V - Vitals Screen
Tab - Inventory
Slash (-/) - Open Chat
M1 (With empty hands) - Attack

Gun:
M1 - Shoot
R - Reload
B - Change Fire Mode

Other:
SpaceBar (on wall, hold) - Climb
SpaceBar (in air, up to 2 times) - Air Jump
LShift (on floor, hold) - Run
LCtrl, C (in air, with momentum) - Dive
LCtrl,C and LShift (on floor, hold) - Slide
