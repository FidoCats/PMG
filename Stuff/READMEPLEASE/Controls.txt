Basic:
WASD - Movement
MouseMove - Look Around
SpaceBar - Jump
LCtrl, C - Crouch
E - Interact
Esc - Menu
M - SpawnMenu
J - MouseLock Toggle
K - HurtBind
L - Ragdoll
LAlt - Freeze Held Object
G - Throwable
H - Switch Throwable
O - Camera Further
I - Camera Closer
P - Reset Camera Distance
Tab - Inventory
Slash (-, /) - Open Chat

Gun:
M1 - Shoot
M2 - Pump / Secondary Function
R - Reload
B - Change Fire Mode

Other:
SpaceBar (in air) - Air Jump
LShift (on floor, hold) - Run
LCtrl, C (in air, with momentum) - Dive
LCtrl, C and LShift (on floor, hold) - Slide
RMB (if a wall is in front and looking up) - Cling / Ledge Grab
RMB (with momentum, with a wall to the side / walking on the side of the wall) - Wall Run
